sort1 uses: Selection Sort

How do you know? It repeatedly finds the smallest element in the unsorted portion and swaps it with the first unsorted element.

sort2 uses: Merge Sort

How do you know? It divides the list into two halves, sorts them recursively, and merges the sorted halves back together in a merge step.

sort3 uses: Bubble Sort

How do you know? With each complete pass, the largest element moves to the end of the list, resembling a "bubbling" effect.
